Frequently asked questions

Is CareLynx available to use today?

The applications are described as being in development/testing. Please contact CareLynx for the latest confirmed status.

Who is CareLynx being designed for?

The intended ecosystem includes patients, hospitals, pharmacies and laboratories.

How can my organisation connect with CareLynx?

Use the partnership inquiry form to get in touch. Messages are sent through our Google Apps Script email endpoint after setup.

How does CareLynx approach privacy?

Privacy and appropriate, role-based access should be central to the platform. Review the final data practices and legal documents before making specific compliance claims.
